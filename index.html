About

I am a PhD student in signal processing and deep learning at Ansbach University of Applied Sciences, Germany. My research focuses on deep learning for EEG analysis, in particular automated epileptic seizure detection, interictal epileptiform discharge detection, low-density EEG setups and dimension reduction methods such as Dynamical Component Analysis (DyCA).

In 2025 I spent a DAAD-funded research stay at the Institut de Neurosciences des Systèmes (Aix-Marseille Université), working with The Virtual Brain. I also teach courses on computer science and an introduction to artificial intelligence.
